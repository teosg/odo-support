Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?
On your iPhone and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud account. We can't see it.

How do I get my data on a new iPhone?
Sign in with the same Apple Account and keep iCloud turned on for Odo. Your cars and history sync automatically.

What does Odo Pro include?
Unlimited cars, every service type, custom service types and data export. The free version covers one car and five common services.

How do I cancel Odo Pro?
In iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Odo Pro. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal.

I bought Odo Pro but it isn't unlocked.
Open Settings in Odo › Upgrade to Odo Pro › Restore Purchases, using the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Safety notice

Odo's service and tyre estimates are based only on the dates and mileage you enter. They are guides, not safety assessments. Always have a qualified workshop check your car and tyres.
