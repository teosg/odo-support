Support · Privacy Policy

Odo is made by Alex Teo, an independent developer in Singapore ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Odo app.

The short version

Odo does not collect your personal data. Your cars, mileage, services, costs, workshops and ratings stay on your iPhone and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud account. We cannot see them. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Data you enter

Everything you add to Odo — cars, odometer readings, service records, costs, workshops, ratings, tyre details and photos — is stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, it is also synced to your private iCloud database through Apple's CloudKit so it appears on your other devices and survives a new phone. Apple stores this data for you; it is not sent to us and we have no access to it. You can delete it at any time by deleting it in the app, or by removing Odo's data in iPhone Settings › your name › iCloud.

Camera and photos

If you choose to scan your odometer or add a car photo, the image is processed on your device using Apple's Vision framework. Photos you add are stored with your data as described above. Odo never uploads images to us.

Workshop search

When you search for a workshop, your search text is sent to Apple Maps to find matching places. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. Odo does not request your location.

Reminders

Service reminders are notifications scheduled on your device. No server is involved.

Purchases

Odo Pro subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple. We do not receive your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether you have an active subscription.

Children

Odo is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Safety notice

Odo's service and tyre estimates are based only on the dates and mileage you enter. They are guides, not safety assessments. Always have a qualified workshop check your car and tyres. We accept no responsibility for any damage, injury or death arising from reliance on these estimates.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
